Why Donate Blood?

🩸 Save Lives

Each blood donation can save up to three lives. Your generosity helps patients in surgeries, trauma, and cancer treatment.

💪 Improve Your Health

Regular blood donation stimulates red cell production and keeps your iron levels balanced.

🤝 Build a Better Community

Donating blood strengthens the bond in your community and encourages others to do the same.

Myths & Facts About Blood Donation

Myth: Donating blood makes you weak.

Fact: Your body replaces the lost fluids within 24 hours, and red blood cells are replenished within weeks.

Myth: Only older people can donate blood.

Fact: Anyone between 18 and 65 years, who is healthy, can donate blood safely.

Myth: Blood donation is painful.

Fact: The only pain you feel is a quick needle prick — the process is safe and monitored by professionals.
